## Ownership and retained evidence

The production collector reads the exact bounded `ss -H -lunpe` command. It
requires stable PID start time, exact runner service cgroup membership and two
FD-to-UDP-inode checks. TCP ownership, process names and PID/FD without inode
never explain UDP. The current head supplies its committed plan marker, unit
hashes, immutable incarnation and one active reservation through coherent
read-only control inventory. Both measured runtime mappings, exact container
and unit identity and pasta must agree before a UDP socket carries an app owner.

Status and diagnosis consume the collector through the shared `udpready.Check`.
A local ready result requires one exact owned socket, no other overlap, a present
bind address, the exact mapping and unchanged units. HTTP success does not repair
missing UDP evidence. Stopped units require measured absence and keep reservations.
Remote is always unverified. No UDP or application-protocol packet is sent.

Released reservation history is read separately, scoped by app and bounded to
64 rows plus one overflow row. It never enters mutable planning ownership. A
history overflow or incomplete socket observation cannot prove absence. Removed
heads and older incarnations remain visible to diagnosis. A socket explained by
another current live reservation is not a historical app's unexplained remnant.
This ownership explanation does not require the current publication to be ready.
A missing or unobserved bind address can make that publication unready without
changing whose socket it is.

## Physical limit

P08-03 physically proved arm64 IPv4/IPv6 mapping, pasta, unit/container identity,
HTTP health and TCP ownership. Its retained UDP inode assertion failed. The Pi's
`6.18.39+rpt-rpi-v8` kernel has no `CONFIG_INET_UDP_DIAG` or `udp_diag` module.
Actual `ss -H -lunpe` output has UDP PID/FD but no inode. This source refuses
ready for that exact shape. It does not substitute a weaker ownership proof.
